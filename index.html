Introduction

In brief

I’m an anthropologist and writer interested in how people cooperate, care for each other, and make sense of radical change – from kinship and childcare to artificial intelligence and global crises.

This site gathers a selection of my writing, research, and miscellaneous projects. It’s a kind of quiet weekend supplement: essays in the front, research in the middle, experiments in the back.
